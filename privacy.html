Privacy Policy

Cureosity

Effective Date: February 25, 2026

Cureosity (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Cureosity app may collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. What Cureosity Does

Cureosity provides educational and supportive content to help users better understand cancer-related topics. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnoses or treatment recommendations.

2. Information We Collect

A) Information you provide

  • Support emails: If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.
  • Optional inputs in the app (if used): Notes, check-ins, or symptom/pain tracking entries you choose to enter.

B) Automatically collected information

  • Basic app diagnostics (optional): Crash logs or performance data if enabled on your device or in the app.

If your current version of Cureosity does not collect any in-app personal data or analytics, that’s okay — this policy still covers support contact and basic diagnostics.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve the app’s features and user experience
  • To respond to support requests
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and fix bugs

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information in limited situations, such as:

  • Service providers that help operate the app (e.g., hosting, crash reporting), if used
  • Legal requirements if we must comply with applicable laws

5. Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described above (for example, to resolve a support issue), unless a longer retention period is required by law.

6. Your Choices

  • You can contact us to request deletion of support emails you have sent us.
  • If the app includes stored entries (like notes/check-ins), you can delete them within the app (if that feature exists).
  • You can control device-level sharing of analytics/crash data in your iOS settings.
